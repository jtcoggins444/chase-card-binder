Price history is your own, recorded daily since October 11, 2026. Prices move daily; check the TCGplayer link before buying or selling. Card pictures load from the Pokémon TCG API and Scrydex image servers, so they need an internet connection. "Full Art" here means cards printed as Ultra Rare / Rare Ultra (full-art EX, GX, V and ex, plus Sword & Shield alt arts). Black White Rares are the top chase cards in Black Bolt and White Flare; Mega Hyper Rares are the gold Mega ex cards; Mega Attack Rares are from Ascended Heroes. VMAX/VSTAR covers every version printed in main sets, plus Shining Fates' Shiny Vault and Celebrations; Trainer Gallery covers Brilliant Stars through Silver Tempest. Rainbow covers Sun & Moon GX rainbows; Gold & Secret covers the secret-rare Pokémon from Black & White through Sword & Shield. Trainer cards, promo cards and 30th Celebration aren't included.
